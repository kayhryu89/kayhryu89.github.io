Data rules

One experiment per fit, at least six points and unique time values. Pressure and sigma are optional (sigma must be positive when weighted). Branch applies globally unless mixed mode is enabled; mixed trajectories are fitted by branch, never pooled. Units are labels, never automatically converted.

02 · Equilibrium coupling

No equilibrium model imported yet.

Imported equilibrium uses the original scaled isotherm coefficients. Pressure and loading units must match its report exactly. A pressure row is required at every kinetic time point; q*(t) follows the chosen linear or zero-order-hold interpolation between sampled equilibrium values.

03 · Candidate models

Weber–Morris is a separate uptake-vs-√t diagnostic, excluded from automatic selection. PFO and constant-q* LDF are equivalent and are not double-counted.
